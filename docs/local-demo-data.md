This is the canonical coverage manifest for the deterministic `EdiSpark Local` workspace. Every user-facing feature change must assess and, when necessary, update this document, its fixture reconciler, and its behavior tests.

The goal is not a large synthetic database. The goal is a small, safe set of connected examples that lets a developer exercise every supported journey immediately after `pnpm dev`.

## Workspace identity

Outbound readiness now validates the current canonical UBL against the bundled OASIS schema graph, the exact active partner route and pinned profile, and mapping coverage before retaining delivery EDI or submitting transport. A readiness check creates no files or exchanges; Send repeats it and records validation evidence on failure. Existing final documents and published mappings remain historical records and may now correctly require review.

The stable tenant draft `readiness:order-response:repeated-lines` (deterministic UUID) demonstrates repeated order-response lines for TRADACOMS. It is deliberately unpublished: populated fields outside its eight base rules must be mapped or explicitly omitted with a business reason and passing fixture before publication. The reconciler restores it when absent and preserves user edits. Existing global revision 1 definitions and active agreement pins are unchanged.

The Documents send panel separates schema finalization from partner readiness and directs document owners to a linked correction draft and administrators to an immutable mapping revision. Correction drafts have their own partner metadata, retain fields outside the business form, and link across sets with `CORRECTS`. Original document bytes remain retained. Local exchange/MFT/configuration metadata now uses the deployment DynamoDB repositories, preserving new operational history and idempotency across restarts; older in-memory history cannot be recreated from missing events. Regression coverage: `JsonSchemaDocumentValidatorTest`, `CorrectionContentTest`, `ExchangeServiceTest`, `MappingRuntimeTest`, `MappingServiceTest`, `EdiPayloadWriterTest`, and `LocalIntegrationDemoSeederTest`.

Validation and activity views group repeated occurrences into business fields and retain exact paths behind technical evidence. An administrator can fork a mapping and load/test the exact retained source without reconstructing it; changed source identities are rejected. Successful processed AS2 receipts update the exchange delivery projection once. Functional-acknowledgement routes remain pending after an MDN. Generated artifacts use per-exchange variant keys so distinct attempts never overwrite or collide with historical representations.

## Adding or changing a feature

The document activity timeline reuses retained Integration exchange stages and mapping warnings in both the Documents inspector and the Order, Invoice and operational-document pages. It numbers events chronologically, retains multiple exchanges, exposes precise timestamps and recorded elapsed time, and offers mapping findings/report links and routing identifiers on demand. No new persisted state or fixtures are introduced by this presentation change. Verify against a retained needs-review exchange through the authenticated local BFF and both UI surfaces; `exchange-timeline.test.ts` covers ordering, equal timestamps, multiple exchanges, document identity and absent timestamps/history.

A feature change is complete only when one of these is true:

1. This table identifies its representative journey, stable fixture keys, reconciler, and behavior evidence; or
2. the change has no observable demo-data impact, and the handoff explicitly says why.

For data-bearing changes:

- create realistic, comprehensive user-facing examples for success and operationally important non-success states; focused unit-test inputs may remain minimal when they are not exposed as product samples;
- use generic placeholders instead of real PII;
- reconcile by stable business identity, not by row count;
- repair missing fixtures and migrate stale fixture shapes without deleting unrelated local data;
- prove creation, repair, and repeat-run idempotence in behavior tests;
- verify the fixture through the real API or UI, not only by inspecting storage.
- add local-only interactive simulations to the shared local tools dock rather than creating independent floating controls.

Update this manifest in the same change whenever a journey, status, entity, or fixture key changes.

## Tenant-scoped storage upgrade

The stable tenant mapping and fixture business keys are unchanged. Documents now stores authenticated ciphertext under `tenants/{tenantId}/content` within its configured local content directory, with one disposable owner-only `.tenant-root.key`. Startup first ensures/backfills the tenant schema-listing index, then copies retained document/schema references from the legacy content directory into this tenant namespace, then reconciles named fixtures. Original plaintext files and developer records are retained; runtime reads never fall back to plaintext. This does not require `pnpm local:reset`. Public bundled schema resources remain classpath reference data.

Verification covers content round trips, duplicate hashes in separate tenants, independent deletion, wrong-resource/tenant decryption, missing/corrupt migration receipts, and seeder creation/repair/idempotence. Live browser sign-in and a deployment migration are separate verification boundaries. See [migration instructions](architecture/documents-storage-migration.md).
