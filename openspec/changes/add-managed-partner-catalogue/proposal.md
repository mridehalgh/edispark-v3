## Why

System administrators need to publish reusable retailer and supplier workflows so each organisation can provision its own trading partner and AS2 connection from versioned settings.

## What changes

- Add a platform-managed catalogue of partner profiles, transport policy and pinned document routes.
- Let organisation administrators apply a published revision to create tenant-owned partner, AS2 connection and agreement drafts.
- Require retailer acceptance and the existing qualification checks before enabling a managed live agreement.
- Provide Tesco UK depot purchase order, invoice and ASN profiles and the B&Q full-acceptance ACK profile. Include Northstar and Harbour in the local catalogue.
